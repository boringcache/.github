# BoringCache Governance

BoringCache is a product of BoringTech Ltd. The company is the project steward,
and Gaurav Tiwari is the lead maintainer.

## How decisions are made

Maintainers seek clear problem statements, user evidence, technical evidence,
and review from the people responsible for affected surfaces. Discussion and
consensus are preferred when practical. BoringTech Ltd retains final authority
over product direction, compatibility, security, release, branding, and
commercial decisions.

Public issues and pull requests inform decisions but are not a vote or a promise
that a proposal will be accepted. Maintainers may defer or decline work that
does not fit the product, cannot be operated safely, or creates an unsupported
long-term contract.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review issues and changes, protect security and compatibility
  boundaries, manage releases, moderate community spaces, and appoint or remove
  other maintainers.
- **Contributors** improve code, documentation, tests, benchmarks, and issue
  reports. Sustained, trusted contribution may lead to broader repository
  permissions at the maintainers' discretion.

Repository-specific ownership and release rules take precedence over these
organization defaults. A public repository may be a distribution, evidence, or
integration surface and therefore accept a narrower class of changes.

## Releases and security

Maintainers decide when a release is ready and which artifacts are supported.
Release notes and repository documentation describe the public contract.
Potential vulnerabilities are handled privately under the
[security policy](https://boringcache.com/security), not through the ordinary
issue process.

## Conduct and changes to governance

Participation in BoringCache community spaces is governed by the
[Code of Conduct](https://github.com/boringcache/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Material changes to this governance model are reviewed publicly in this
repository before taking effect, except where a private security or legal
matter requires confidential handling.
